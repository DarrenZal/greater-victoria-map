# Data sources

All data in this folder was retrieved on **2026-10-21** and processed by `scripts/fetch-layers.sh` (mapshaper 0.7.93). Every geometry is clipped to the demo box `-123.75,48.28,-123.20,48.70` (west, south, east, north), simplified for the web and rounded to 5 decimal places (about 1 m). These are orientation layers, not survey or legal boundaries.

These notes cover the data. The MIT licence in [LICENSE](../LICENSE) covers the code only, and every layer below keeps its own licence.

Licence statement for every row below that says "OGL-BC": **Contains information licensed under the [Open Government Licence - British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content?id=A519A56BC2BF44E4A008B33FCF527F61).** The licence has a non-endorsement clause. ## Filters and processing per layer

Feature counts are after clipping to the demo box. The count before clipping is in brackets where it differs.

- **municipalities**: rows where `ADMIN_AREA_GROUP_NAME` is "Capital Regional District". Keeps the abbreviation as `name`. Simplify 8%, keep shapes. Legal boundaries include adjacent water, and the popup says so.
- **bc-parks**: all 17 provincial parks and ecological reserves in the box. Keeps `PROTECTED_LANDS_NAME`, `PROTECTED_LANDS_DESIGNATION` and `PARK_CLASS`. Names are the Province's published names, displayed as published (capitals and the dual name `ȽÁUˌWELṈEW̱ / JOHN DEAN PARK` unchanged). Simplify 15%, keep shapes.
- **greenspaces**: rows where `REGIONAL_DISTRICT` is "Capital" and `PARK_TYPE` is not "Private" (1,111 rows), then the 79 rows that carry the Capital Regional District statement (`lic` "C") are dropped, leaving 1,032 (75 of those 79 would have remained after clipping and sliver removal). Slivers under 150 sq m and features left empty by clipping are dropped, leaving 934. Then the one row that carries the Islands Trust statement (`lic` "I") is dropped, leaving 933. The fetch script now drops both "C" and "I" before clipping. The Islands Trust row was removed from the committed file by filtering it out directly, without a refetch, and its pre-clipping count was not recomputed. A full refetch drops it earlier, and because the simplifier ranks vertices across the whole layer, it can differ from the committed file by a few vertices in other parks. Keeps `name`, `type`, `use`, `muni` and `lic`. Simplify 20%, keep shapes. The simplifier reports 65 line intersections it could not repair, where parks overlap in the source. That is harmless for translucent fills.
- **streams**: named segments only (`GNIS_NAME` not empty), 592 before clipping and 578 after. Keeps `name` and `order` (stream order). Simplify 40%, keep shapes. The Freshwater Atlas has 12 named Bowker Creek segments (about 2.1 km of a creek the 2012 Bowker Creek Blueprint measures at 9.4 km), because piped stretches are unnamed or unmapped. The fetch also keeps `EDGE_TYPE`, `BLUE_LINE_KEY` and `DOWNSTREAM_ROUTE_MEASURE` in `raw/` (not committed), for the flow lines below.
- **streams-flow**: built from the clipped, simplified streams by `scripts/make-flow.mjs`. Three steps. (1) Direction: in the Freshwater Atlas the first vertex of a segment is its downstream end, so vertex order runs upstream. Measured on 2026-10-21 from the vertex heights of the raw download (592 named segments): the last vertex is higher than the first on 401, lower on 4, within half a metre on 187. Every line is reversed once, so the last vertex is downstream, which is the way MapLibre marches the dashes. The script re-checks the assumption on each run, using the distance from the mouth, and stops if it fails. (2) Joins: segments of one stream with the same stream order that share an end point become one continuous line, so the dash pattern does not restart at every join. 533 segments became 124 lines. (3) Open flow only: edge types 1000 (single line main flow), 1050 (through a wetland), 1200 (construction line main flow, through a lake) and 1250 (construction line, double-line river, main flow). Left out, so they get no dashes: 1425 "Construction line, subsurface flow" (11 segments, 1.3 km, Bowker Creek and Swan Lake Creek), 1450 "Construction line, connection" (11 segments, 3.0 km) and 2000 "Single line, Canal" (23 segments, 11.6 km, eleven creeks). Edge type names are from the [Freshwater Atlas User Guide](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/data/geographic/topography/fwa/fwa_user_guide.pdf), table 2. The kept lines are 319 of the 335 km of named creeks in the box. Bowker Creek keeps about 480 m of its 2,114 m (the rest is 32% subsurface and 45% canal), and its mouth reach is among them. The page checks the direction at two mouths: the last vertex of the Colquitz River line must be within 300 m of Portage Inlet (-123.41134, 48.45947) and the last vertex of the Bowker Creek line within 300 m of the Oak Bay shore (-123.30658, 48.42922).
- **lakes**: 1 ha or more, 80 before clipping and 79 after. Keeps `name` and `area_ha`. Simplify 30%, keep shapes.
- **wetlands**: 0.5 ha or more, 205 before clipping and 203 after. Keeps `name` and `area_ha`. Simplify 30%, keep shapes.
- **watersheds**: named watersheds under 8,000 ha whose centroid is inside `-123.65,48.34,-123.22,48.66`. Keeps `name` and `area_ha` (rounded). Simplify 12%, keep shapes. Watersheds nest, so the page shows the smallest one under a click. The Province's Bowker Creek polygon is 1,381 ha. Other sources give other figures, so the popup says other groups draw watersheds differently.
- **bec**: 14 clipped polygons dissolved to one per biogeoclimatic label: CDF mm, CWH dm 1, CWH dm 2, CWH mm 1, CWH mm 2. Simplify 4%, keep shapes. The polygons extend over open water, so the page redraws the basemap's water above them.

**Open decision: the 532 rows with no provider statement.** Either read each municipality's open data terms, or ship only rows with an explicit OGL statement by adding `-filter 'lic!=null'` the same way. That would leave 401 rows (156 Victoria and 245 Saanich). Until then they are kept on the Province's licence alone.

**Restoring Islands Trust.** Read the live [terms page](https://islandstrust.bc.ca/mapping-resources/mapping/terms-and-conditions-of-use/) from a network that can reach it and confirm it grants an open licence that allows copying and publishing with attribution. If it does: (1) change the filter in `scripts/fetch-layers.sh` to `lic!="C"` and run it (`FETCH=1`, or `FETCH=0` if `raw/` is still there), which brings the row back; (2) put the `I` entry back in `LIC_STATEMENTS` in `js/app.js` with the statement the page specifies, and add the provider to `PARKS_ATTRIB`; (3) add the licence line and link back to the credits list in `index.html`; (4) in `scripts/check-demo.mjs`, add the statement to `PROVIDER_STATEMENTS`, add `islandstrust.bc.ca` to `ALLOWED_HOSTS`, and remove the "no Islands Trust rows" check and its self-test; (5) update the counts here and in the README. If it does not, leave the row out.

The page shows each kept provider's statement in the credits panel and, word for word, in the popup of any park from that provider. The on-map attribution line carries the Province's statement and a short form of the provider list.

## Terrain shading (not stored)

No elevation data is stored in this folder. The Terrain shading layer draws a hillshade in the browser from [AWS Terrain Tiles](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/) in terrarium encoding, loaded from `https://s3.amazonaws.com/elevation-tiles-prod/terrarium/{z}/{x}/{y}.png`. The layer is off by default and no tile is requested until the box is ticked. The tiles are a composite. The `x-amz-meta-x-imagery-sources` header on Victoria-area tiles checked on 2026-10-21 names GMTED2010 and ETOPO1 at low zoom, then nrcan_cdem (Canada), srtm, USGS ned13, and at the eastern edge of the box ned19 San Juan County lidar. The attribution lines the page shows, from the [tile attribution notes](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md) ("Attribution is required for many terrain tile data providers. Example language is provided below, but you are responsible for researching each project to follow their license terms."):

- Canada terrain data contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada.
- United States 3DEP (formerly NED) and global GMTED2010 and SRTM terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.
- Global ETOPO1 terrain data U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.

The "Mapzen" line applies only "when using Mapzen's hosted service", not this bucket. Two cautions. The tile notes say the S3 tiles "are meant for efficient networking with EC2 resources only" and are not cached by a CDN, so a busy public site should host its own pre-extracted tiles. And a visitor who ticks the box sends tile requests to Amazon S3, which the credits panel says.

## Live river gauges (not added)

Checked on 2026-10-21 against `https://api.weather.gc.ca/collections/hydrometric-stations/items?bbox=-123.75,48.28,-123.20,48.70&f=json`: 26 stations in the demo box, all with status "Discontinued" and `REAL_TIME` 0, and `hydrometric-realtime` returns 0 items for the box. The nearest live gauges are in the Cowichan valley, outside it. The API answers browsers (`Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`), but the API's own licence field names the "Environment and Climate Change Canada Data Server End-use Licence" (https://eccc-msc.github.io/open-data/licence/readme_en/), while ECCC's catalogue pages name OGL-Canada. A layer would be empty and its licence is not cleanly OGL-Canada, so none was built.
