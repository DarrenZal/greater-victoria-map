Water

Dashes show which way creeks drain, from the Province's stream lines. They do not show how much water is flowing. Canals get dashes too. Reaches mapped as pipes or connection lines do not.

Flow is off because your device asks for less motion.

Land

Tilted view. Drag with the right mouse button, or with two fingers, to rotate and tilt. Tick Terrain shading as well for light and shade on the hills.

Only records with an open licence. A dot with no figures is a location, not an empty site. Brown: landfills and waste sites. Blue: wastewater. Yellow: power.

Rows with no provider statement are covered by the Province's licence above. Parks run by the Capital Regional District are not on this map yet.

Flow. The moving dashes show the direction creeks drain, taken from the Province's stream lines. They do not show how much water is flowing or how fast. Canals get dashes too. Reaches the Province maps as underground pipes or connection lines have no dashes.

Terrain shading (off until you switch it on). Hillshade drawn in your browser from AWS Terrain Tiles. Canada terrain data contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada. United States 3DEP (formerly NED) and global GMTED2010 and SRTM terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. Global ETOPO1 terrain data U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration. The full list of sources is in the tile attribution notes.

3D terrain (off until you switch it on). It lifts the map from the same elevation tiles as Terrain shading, so the credits above apply to it too.

A dot with no figures is a location, not an empty site. Figures are labelled as reported, as capacity or as modelled. Sites from sources with unclear or share-alike licences are left out.

Live data from Environment and Climate Change Canada (radar, weather alerts, air quality, current conditions and river gauges; each off until you switch it on). Data Source: Environment and Climate Change Canada. Contains information licenced under the Data Server End-use Licence of Environment and Climate Change Canada. Where a weather station or gauge is run by another body, its popup names that body. Weather alerts are shown exactly as issued. This map is not an alerting service and is not for safety decisions: use Environment Canada's own alerts.

Earthquakes (off until you switch it on). Credit: U.S. Geological Survey. USGS-authored or produced data and information are considered to be in the U.S. Public Domain. Early magnitudes and positions are revised.

Life observations (off until you switch it on). A sample of up to 300 occurrence records from GBIF, dated in the last 12 months. Only records licensed CC0 or CC BY 4.0 are kept, and records withheld or generalised by their publisher are dropped. Positions are rounded to about 1 km. Each popup keeps the record's dataset, licence and source link.

Example place. The Bowker Creek pin is an example, not an event. Its figures are from the 2012 Bowker Creek Blueprint, linked in the pin. Its position is a point on the creek from OpenStreetMap.

Please note. Boundaries are for orientation. They are simplified for the web and are not survey or legal boundaries. Creeks that run in pipes may be missing or partly mapped. It is not endorsed by the Province of British Columbia or any data provider.

Privacy. This page uses no analytics and no cookies. Map tiles load from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), so that service receives your browser's tile requests. If you switch on Terrain shading or 3D terrain, elevation tiles load from Amazon Web Services (s3.amazonaws.com), which then receives those requests too. Nothing else loads until you switch a layer on. The live layers then send your browser's requests, with your internet address, to the service behind each: Environment and Climate Change Canada for radar, alerts, air quality, conditions and gauges (api.weather.gc.ca and geo.weather.gc.ca), the U.S. Geological Survey for earthquakes (earthquake.usgs.gov) and GBIF for life observations (api.gbif.org). Ecoregions and Industry and waste sites load a file from this site only.
